# business-cases/

**Presentable** material: what I'd hand to a client or show in an interview.
Everything is **fictional** — no real clients, nothing from my job.

| Subfolder | What it holds |
|---|---|
| [`cases/`](./cases) | Cases solved with a fixed method: what I check, what I contain, what I preserve and who I notify. |
| [`policies-and-matrix/`](./policies-and-matrix) | The surface × control matrix, policies and checklists. |
| [`slides/`](./slides) | Weekly slides and milestone decks. |
| [`soc/`](./soc) | Playbooks, challenge write-ups and incident cases. |
| [`assessment-cane/`](./assessment-cane) | The final security assessment, treating [Cane](https://github.com/0x0128/cane) as if it were a client. |
